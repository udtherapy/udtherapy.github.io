How therapy can help

Support for the challenges families carry together.

✦

Process the Effects of Caregiving

Caregiver fatigue and burnout is valid, and therapy offers a safe space to process these shifting roles, new emotions, and determine a path forward with the caregivers needs in mind.

✦

Age Appropriate Psychoeducation

Therapy can help to support younger family members with age appropriate conversations about the changes to their family unit, understanding mental or physical illness of a loved one, and reiterate their own importance in the family.

✦

Trauma-Informed Care

Your therapist can support you in working through complex grief, trauma, and difficult transitions with researched backed therapy modalities such as CBT, DBT, Narrative, and Existential Therapy.
