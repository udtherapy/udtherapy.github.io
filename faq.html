FAQ

Questions you may have before we begin.

Learn more about who I work with, the concerns I support, and what to expect from telehealth counseling.

Who do you work with?

I provide individual counseling for children, teens, adults, and elders, as well as counseling for families together.

Do you offer virtual therapy?

Yes. I offer telehealth sessions to clients in Texas.

What is reverse caregiving?

It describes the complex shift when adult children take on caregiving responsibilities for aging or disabled parents. Therapy can help with burnout, grief, family conflict, and changing roles.

Do you support families affected by mental health or addiction challenges?

Yes. Family members can work together to process grief, conflict, and the weight of these challenges and transitions.

What approaches do you use?

I draw on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), narrative therapy, and person-centered work, tailored to each client.

Are you accepting new clients?

Yes. I’m currently accepting new clients. Please use the contact page to reach out about getting started.
