FAQ

Questions you may have before we begin.

Learn more about who I work with, the concerns I support, and what to expect from counseling.

What ages do you work with?

I provide individual counseling for individuals 6+, as well as family therapy. For children ages 6-10, I only provide in-person sessions.

Do you offer in-person therapy?

Yes, but at the moment there is a waitlist for in-person sessions. Please reach out if you are interested, as it is expected to open up soon.

What approaches do you use?

I draw on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), narrative therapy, and person-centered work, tailored to each client.

What do you specialize in?

I specialize in working with families affected by mental health and addiction challenges, grief, and life transitions. Family members can work together or individually in session to process these experiences.

Do you accept insurance?

We are not currently accepting insurance, but we can provide documentation for out-of-network reimbursement if needed.

Are you accepting new clients?

Yes. I’m currently accepting new virtual clients. Please use the contact page to reach out about getting started.
