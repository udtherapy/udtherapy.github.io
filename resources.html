Substance use treatment information

SAMHSA’s National Helpline provides free, confidential treatment referral and information for mental health and substance use concerns, 24 hours a day.

Visit SAMHSA Call 1-800-662-HELP (4357)

Support for older adults and caregivers

The Eldercare Locator connects older adults and families with local services and Area Agencies on Aging.

Visit Eldercare Locator Call 1-800-677-1116
