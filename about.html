About

Meet Bethanney Krueger.

MA, LPC-Associate, NCC · Counseling for individuals and families across the lifespan.

I’m Bethanney Krueger, MA, LPC-Associate, NCC. I work with people navigating caregiving, grief, family strain, and the transitions that can make life feel especially heavy.

I earned my Master of Arts in Professional Counseling at Texas State University. My clinical training includes counseling adults in an inpatient psychiatric setting, supporting children and families through grief, and providing counseling to adults and young children through a university counseling center.

I also hold bachelor’s degrees in English and Secondary Education. My earlier work in education continues to inform how I support children, teens, and families.

As a Texas Licensed Professional Counselor-Associate, I practice under the supervision of Katelyn Williams, MA, LPC-S, RPT-S, NCC.

  • Specialized support for adult children caring for aging or disabled parents
  • Family support through serious mental health and addiction challenges
  • Individual counseling for children, teens, adults, and elders
