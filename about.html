Meet Bethanney Krueger.

I’m Bethanney Krueger, MA, LPC-Associate, NCC. My journey to becoming a therapist has been shaped by a lifelong curiosity about people, their experiences, and the stories that shape how they see the world.

Before earning my master’s in Professional Counseling from Texas State University, I worked as an English teacher, where I developed a deep appreciation for the different perspectives, experiences, and stories each person brings with them. Since then, my clinical experience has taken me into a variety of settings, supporting people through some of life’s most difficult moments.

During my internship at Austin State Hospital, I worked with individuals experiencing severe mental illness and addiction. This experience deepened my understanding of the complexities of mental health and reinforced the importance of approaching each person with compassion rather than judgment. At the Christi Center, a grief support nonprofit in Austin, I worked with both children and adults navigating grief and loss, gaining a greater appreciation for the unique ways we experience and make sense of our relationships.

I believe that while we may not be able to control all the circumstances of our lives, we have a say in how we respond to them and the lives we build around them. There are many variables that shape the way in which we all view the world, and therapy is a space to slow down and get curious about these variables, while figuring out which ones are helping us grow, and which we have outgrown. Together, we can explore what matters to you, make room for the parts of life that feel complicated or out of control, and build practical skills for navigating difficult emotions, relationships, and change.

My goal is to create a space where you feel safe bringing up your experiences, thoughts, and feelings without fear of judgment. Whether you're working through difficult emotions, complicated relationships, or feelings you may not fully understand yourself, we'll approach them with curiosity and compassion. Together, we can explore what matters to you and find a way forward that feels authentic to your needs and values.

As a Texas Licensed Professional Counselor-Associate, I practice under the supervision of Katelyn Williams, MA, LPC-S, RPT-S, NCC.
