Services

Support for caregiving and family challenges.

Whether you’re caring for a parent, supporting a loved one through serious mental health or addiction challenges, or navigating grief and conflict, therapy can help you find clarity and resilience.

Ways to work together

Care tailored to each person and family.

Individual Therapy

The client and therapist meet one-on-one to explore personal challenges, emotions, and goals. Each session is 60 minutes long, focused on your unique needs and experiences.

Family Therapy

A parent and child meet with the therapist together to address family challenges, with a focus on improving family functioning and communication. The therapist may also meet individually with each family member before the family sessions to ensure everyone feels heard and supported.

Play Therapy

For children 6-10 years old, play therapy provides a safe and engaging way to express feelings, work through challenges, and develop coping skills in the natural environment of play. Periodically, the therapist then also meets with the caregiver to discuss progress and ensure continuity of care.

What to Expect

The Therapy Process

1

Reach out

Reach out to share what kind of support you’re looking for and ask any initial questions. We can discuss availability, fees, and logistics before the first session.

2

Initial Meeting

In our first few sessions, we’ll discuss your concerns and strengths while creating goals and getting to know each other.

3

Working

In the following weeks, we'll work together to explore your concerns and implement strategies for growth, checking in on personal goals and collaborating on any new developments.

4

Move forward

As you progress, we'll continue to build on the work done together, ensuring you have the tools and support needed to maintain your growth and well-being outside of therapy.
