Services

Support for caregiving and family challenges.

Whether you’re caring for a parent, supporting a loved one through serious mental health or addiction challenges, or navigating grief and conflict, therapy can help you find clarity and resilience.

Reverse caregiving

When an adult child becomes a caregiver for an aging or disabled parent, family roles and relationships can shift. Therapy offers space to process grief, stress, conflict, and caregiver burnout while exploring ways to care for yourself, too.

Family mental health & addiction support

When a family member faces serious mental health or addiction challenges, loved ones may carry grief, worry, conflict, and uncertainty. Family counseling can help members process these experiences together.

Grief, loss & family trauma

Support for children, teens, adults, elders, and families processing loss, trauma, and the difficult emotions that accompany change.

Relationship strain & life transitions

Explore communication difficulties, caregiver burnout, shifting family roles, and the strain of life’s hardest seasons.
